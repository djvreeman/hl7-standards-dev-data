## How to Read This Report

### Key Metrics

- **New:** Issues created during the specified time period
- **Resolved:** Issues with a resolution date during the specified time period. Note: the resolution date in Jira is assigned early -- when an issue is given a (proposed) disposition. This is not necessarily when the change is applied to the specification.
- **Backlog:** Issues created at any time before the end of the specified period that remain unresolved at the end of that period
- **Ave (days):** Average time to resolution for issues resolved in this period
- **Median (days):** Median time to resolution (50% of issues resolved faster than this)
- **P80 (days):** 80th percentile resolution time (80% of issues resolved faster than this)

### Time Periods

Periods are defined as:
- **T1:** January, February, March, April
- **T2:** May, June, July, August
- **T3:** September, October, November, December

### Performance Bands

| Band                  | P80 Range (days) | Interpretation                                                                             |
|-----------------------|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **🏎️ Presto**         | ≤ 60             | 80% of tickets close within two months. Very fast, high performance, hypercar speed.                                      |
| **🚴 Allegro**              | 61 – 180         | 80% close within six months. Fast, responsive, moving quickly.                    |
| **🚶 Andante**         | 181 – 365        | 80% close within a year. Moderate pace, moving steady, but with opportunities to accelerate.         |
| **🐢 Adagio** | > 365            | 20% of tickets take *more* than a year. Very slow. Let's look for bottlenecks or resource gaps.  |

_Note: For fun, these performance band labels are inspired by the music vocabulary for tempo. For more information, see the [Tempo article on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempo)._
